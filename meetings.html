Meetings

Annual General Meeting - AGM

  • The purpose of the AGM is to determine major issues and all senior management members are invited to attend. The meeting is also aimed at enrichment of the relationship among senior managers of the network members
  • Bilateral Meetings will be presented among members
  • Members are encouraged to attend every meeting. Attendance is mandatory once every 2 years. Failure to attend consecutive meetings without good cause may be grounds for termination of membership.
  • Meeting packages are all-inclusive except traveling costs.
