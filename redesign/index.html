Why us

Why Partners Unlimited

A network designed so mid-size forwarders can work as true partners — exclusive by market, accountable to each other, and connected in person.

Exclusive by market

A maximum of 4 members per country (6 in China, the USA and India). In smaller markets the Board may decide that 1 or 2 members will suffice. Members represent the network in their assigned market.

Equal acceptance

Regardless of the market and the size of the company, all members are accepted and treated equally. The network is about connecting people and connecting needs.

Peer evaluation

Every member is objectively evaluated by their peers each year on communication, sales activity, accounting and operation efficiency, and must maintain a minimum score of 50% efficiency.

Financial protection

A compulsory Debt Protection Fund covers members for non-disputed unpaid invoices of another Partners Unlimited member. Funds are built up by yearly member contributions.

Meetings that connect

The Annual General Meeting brings senior management together on major issues and relationships. Global Sales Meetings, held as needed, give sales professionals a platform to meet bilaterally.

Ideas from members

The network must create value for its members, and the best ideas on how to operate it come from the members themselves. People, people, people.

How membership works

Applicants that meet the membership standards may apply online. Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, the applicant may be accepted as a member of the network.

    Apply

    Complete the application form and forward the required documents.

  2. 2

    Board review

    Applications are reviewed by the Board of Directors. The Board may reject an application without comments.

  3. 3

    Join the network

    Subject to approval, the applicant may be accepted as a member and represent the network in their assigned market.
