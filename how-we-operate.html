How We Operate

For better management of growth, the Network will sub-divide the global market into 4 Regions based on geographical region, namely: The Americas / The Asia – Pacific / The Africa-Mideast / The EU

Qualified members are invited to the Advisory Board to represent the views and interests of the members. These members will form the Management Board of Directors holding positions as Regional Directors for the 4 Regions.

Active members of the Association must be private enterprises or corporations, involved in the Freight Forwarding Industry and actively supporting the purpose and objectives of the Association.

Applicants that meet the membership standards may apply in writing or online to the Chairman of the Board for membership. Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, the applicant may be accepted as a member to the network. The Board may reject an application without comments.

Membership of the Association entitles each member to represent the Association within the assigned market in which the member resides and conducts business.

Any member may resign by giving three months’ notice to the Board of Directors, by the end of the month. The annual membership fee remains due and no refund is granted. Members of the Association may only promote the offices where they are assigned within the network.

Members may be disqualified based on the following

  • Conduct that would disgrace or go against the network
  • Consecutive performance below expectations for 3 years
  • Criminal offence
  • Failure to settle the Annual Membership dues.
  • Failure to settle payments to other members
