Partners Unlimited may remove, hide, or edit a listing where we need to, for example if information is inaccurate, consent is withdrawn, or we believe a listing is being misused. We may also update the directory as membership changes.

Directory privacy and listing policy

The public Member Directory is a list of Partners Unlimited members who have agreed to be shown there.

What the public directory shows

A public listing may include:

  • Company name
  • Country
  • City
  • Ports
  • Website
  • Description

No other listing details are published in the public directory.

What stays private

The following information is not published in the public directory. It is used only by Partners Unlimited administrators to run the network:

  • Contact person names
  • Email addresses
  • Membership status
  • Internal notes

Listing with your consent

A member is listed in the public directory only if that member has given consent to be listed. If we have not received consent, the member stays unlisted.

How to update or remove a listing

If you want to update your listing, correct a detail, or have it removed from the public directory, please email us at . A Partners Unlimited administrator will act on the request within 5 working days.

Email addresses and scraping

We do not publish member email addresses in the public directory. Please do not scrape the directory for contact details or use it to send unsolicited messages to members.
